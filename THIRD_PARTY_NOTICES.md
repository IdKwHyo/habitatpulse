## Data and map services

Habitat Pulse also displays remotely supplied data and imagery. Those materials are not redistributed as part of the source package and retain the terms and licenses of their providers:

- [iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org/pages/terms) observations and photographs retain the licenses selected by their contributors.
- [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and available under the Open Data Commons Open Database License.
- [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) weather data is used under the provider's published terms.
- [Nominatim](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/) place search is used under the OpenStreetMap Foundation usage policy.
