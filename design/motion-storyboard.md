# Motion Storyboard

| Scene | Narrative | Pattern | Camera / transition | Mobile cut |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | A city contains unseen life | Pinned hero, 180vh | Slow Z approach; three-line mask reveal | Static field, short line reveals |
| Signal | Nearby records become visible | Sticky narrative, 140vh | Particles expand; field-note labels counter-drift | Normal flow; no counter-parallax |
| Connection — peak | Small spaces become a corridor | 3D procedural orbit, 300vh pinned | Particle morph + camera dolly; deep green to living signal | Static connected formation, no pin |
| Evidence | Real observations in a real place | Data story, 140vh | Map plane rotates from `rotateX(12deg)` to flat | Flat map from start |
| Action | Define one useful patch | Editorial tool, normal flow | Result vessel settles and reacts to choices | Stacked form and result |
| Resolution | A network begins with one space | Landing sequence, 100vh | Corridor recedes; closing copy tracks forward | Static closing frame |

## Pacing

Variable: spacious opening, gradual rise, one intense midpoint, then a calm functional landing. No adjacent scenes share the same reveal or transition treatment.

## Motion budget

- Primary family: Three.js camera and particle morph.
- Supporting family: GSAP line masks and spatial section transitions.
- Interaction family: subtle pointer tilt and press feedback.
- Maximum simultaneous motion types in any 50vh window: three.
