# Cinematic Audit

## Motion personality

Alive, observant, grounded, deliberate, quietly magnetic.

## Emotional arc

- Opening: curiosity — the city contains more life than the visitor notices.
- Rising: recognition — individual sightings become visible signals.
- Peak: connection — scattered observations assemble into a corridor, turning data into habitat logic.
- Proof: agency — the live map and weather show that the story is grounded in a real place.
- Closing: resolve — a small patch can join a larger living network.

## Audience and context

Primary viewers are hackathon judges on desktop and students demonstrating on laptops; the public experience must also remain complete on mobile. Attention is limited, so the concept must become clear in the first viewport and the working tool must appear within three deliberate scroll beats.

## Accessibility target

WCAG 2.2 AA. Reduced motion is a static editorial cut, not a blank fallback. Keyboard access and native scroll remain intact.

## Primary visual system

Botanical cinematography: deep chlorophyll fields, pale lichen reading surfaces, high-contrast editorial type, and procedural bioluminescent particles. Accent system: scientific field-note typography and a live map plane.

## Color progression

Deep forest → luminous green peak → pale field-data surface → deep hopeful close.
