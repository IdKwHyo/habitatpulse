# Art Direction

## World premise

The interface exists inside a dark urban habitat after rain. Each particle is an observation—not a decorative star. At first the points drift separately; through the journey they align into a connective path.

## Hero concept

An asymmetric editorial title, “A city is never only concrete,” enters while the camera approaches a sparse living field. One direct action invites the visitor to read their neighborhood.

## Motif system

- Soft circular observation particles
- Thin orbital traces suggesting range and movement
- A single map plane that passes from atmosphere into interface
- Vertical field-note captions anchored to the viewport edges
- Living-signal green used only for active or connected states

## Material and light

Surfaces are matte and botanical. The light source sits above-left and warms slightly as particles connect. Noise is a 2% CSS texture, not an expensive full-frame shader pass. Glow is local to particles; it never substitutes for hierarchy.

## Signature moment

During the pinned “connection” chapter, the camera moves through scattered points while the particle geometry morphs into a helical corridor. The copy moves in the opposite Z direction, creating a restrained dolly effect. The moment explains the project thesis: isolated small habitats become meaningful when connected.

## Asset sourcing

No stock hero image and no external 3D model. The world is generated procedurally in one Three.js point field. Local species photographs come from the live iNaturalist response and retain their source licenses.
